Biography

Haoran MO is a postdoctoral researcher at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). He received his PhD, MSc, and BEng degrees from Sun Yat-sen University in 2024, 2020, and 2018, respectively. He was invited as a visiting researcher at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Waseda University (Tokyo, Japan) in 2019.

His research interests are sketch-based visual creation, 2D/3D animation, and multi-modal LLM. He has published over 10 papers in top international journals and conferences, including TOG, SIGGRAPH, SIGGRAPH Asia, TVCG, and ECCV. His own open-source repositories on GitHub have received over 5k stars in total. He serves as a program committee member and a session chair for SIGGRAPH Asia 2025, and as a reviewer for conferences SIGGRAPH, SIGGRAPH Asia, Eurographics (EG), etc., and for journals TOG, TVCG, TMM, etc. He was awarded CCF-CAD&CG Excellent Student in 2021.
